# Appendix III: Timeline of Key Events (1994–2026)

Selected milestones in global tech and business history, roughly 3–5 per year, to anchor the reader in time.

---

## 1994

- Netscape founded; Navigator launches, opening the browser era
- Yahoo founded at Stanford; human-curated web directories
- Amazon founded in a Seattle garage; books first
- China connects fully to the global Internet (77th member)

## 1995

- Wang Chuanfu founds BYD in Shenzhen, starting in batteries
- Amazon.com goes live; first book sold
- AuctionWeb (later eBay) founded
- Netscape IPO ignites the dot-com boom
- Sun releases Java; Microsoft ships Windows 95

## 1996

- ICQ launches in Israel
- Page and Brin begin search research at Stanford
- Yahoo IPO
- China's Sina, NetEase, and Sohu portals emerge (1997–1998)

## 1997

- Browser wars; U.S. antitrust scrutiny of Microsoft begins
- Amazon IPO
- Netflix founded (DVD by mail)
- google.com domain registered

## 1998

- Google incorporated in a Menlo Park garage
- Bezos expands Amazon toward the "everything store"
- Tencent founded in Shenzhen; OICQ project starts
- TSMC ramps 0.25µm foundry capacity
- PayPal predecessor Confinity founded

## 1999

- Alibaba founded in Hangzhou (B2B)
- Tencent launches OICQ
- Ctrip founded in Shanghai
- Salesforce founded (SaaS)

## 2000

- Nasdaq peaks near 5,048 in March, then dot-com crash
- Baidu founded in Beijing
- SoftBank invests $20M in Alibaba
- Cisco briefly world's most valuable company

## 2001

- Apple iPod; Wikipedia launches
- Amazon's first quarterly profit
- Microsoft Xbox

## 2002

- eBay acquires PayPal
- Amazon internal infrastructure seeds AWS
- LinkedIn founded
- China's Internet users pass 50 million

## 2003

- Taobao launches (Alibaba C2C)
- Tesla founded
- iTunes Store; Skype
- BYD acquires Qinchuan, enters autos

## 2004

- Facebook at Harvard
- Google IPO (Dutch auction)
- Amazon Prime
- TSMC 90nm; Alipay spun out of Taobao
- ARM Cortex-A8 defines mobile SoC era

## 2005

- YouTube founded; Google buys Android
- Yahoo invests $1B in Alibaba for 40%
- Baidu NASDAQ IPO

## 2006

- Twitter launches
- AWS EC2 and S3 reshape cloud computing
- Tesla Roadster unveiled
- Shopify founded in Ottawa
- Google acquires YouTube

## 2007

- iPhone announced
- Airbnb origin story in San Francisco
- Amazon Kindle
- Qualcomm Snapdragon; Netflix streaming

## 2008

- Uber idea born in Paris
- Apple App Store
- Android 1.0
- Bitcoin white paper

## 2009

- Bitcoin genesis block
- WhatsApp founded
- Alibaba Cloud (Aliyun) founded
- TSMC 40nm

## 2010

- Instagram; iPad
- Stripe; Xiaomi founded
- Tesla IPO
- Uber launches in San Francisco

## 2011

- WeChat launches
- Airbnb major funding round
- Jobs dies; Apple under Cook
- Alibaba splits Taobao / Tmall businesses

## 2012

- ByteDance and Toutiao; Didi founded
- Facebook IPO
- NVIDIA Kepler; TSMC 28nm
- Tesla Model S production ramps

## 2013

- Meituan vs. Dianping; end of "thousand-group" wars
- Bitcoin passes $1,000
- DJI Phantom 2 consumer drones

## 2014

- Alibaba NYSE IPO (record scale)
- Facebook buys WhatsApp
- Amazon Echo / Alexa
- Didi vs. Kuaidi subsidy war
- First ASML EUV tools delivered

## 2015

- OpenAI founded
- Meituan–Dianping merge; Didi–Kuaidi merge
- TSMC 16nm
- Pinduoduo launches (September 2015)

## 2016

- NVIDIA Pascal; AlphaGo vs. Lee Sedol
- Douyin (TikTok China) launches
- Unitree founded
- AWS revenue >$12B
- Tesla acquires SolarCity

## 2017

- Transformer paper (Google)
- Bitcoin near $20K
- NVIDIA Volta / V100
- Tesla Model 3 "production hell"

## 2018

- GDPR; Meituan IPO
- TikTok global downloads surge
- TSMC 7nm
- Tesla "going private" episode

## 2019

- GPT-2; Huawei entity list
- ByteDance valuation peak
- NVIDIA buys Mellanox
- Tesla Shanghai Gigafactory

## 2020

- COVID remote-work boom
- Tesla market cap milestone
- NVIDIA–ARM deal fails on regulation
- TSMC 5nm; BYD Blade Battery

## 2021

- Metaverse hype; Meta rebrand
- NVIDIA $500B+ cap
- Tesla FSD Beta
- CATL trillion-yuan cap
- OpenAI DALL·E / Codex

## 2022

- ChatGPT (November 30)
- Musk acquires Twitter
- TSMC 3nm
- TikTok regulatory pressure; Figure AI founded

## 2023

- GPT-4; NVIDIA $1T cap
- Huawei Mate 60 / Kirin return
- Cybertruck deliveries
- OpenAI board crisis

## 2024

- Multimodal model wave (Sora, Gemini, etc.)
- NVIDIA Blackwell / B200
- Apple Intelligence
- FSD V12 end-to-end push
- OpenAI–Apple integration

## 2025

- Model price wars; inference costs fall
- Humanoid robot pilots (e.g., Figure–BMW)
- TSMC 2nm trials
- Diverging U.S.–China AI regulation
- EV penetration accelerates

## 2026

- Crossroads: does AI kill apps or become the super-plugin?
- Embodied AI and cloud LLMs in parallel
- Dual-core frame: U.S. cloud APIs vs. China's physical execution
- Compute–data–algorithm triangle continues
- (Future entries left open)

---

*Based on historical sources; 2025–2026 entries include ongoing or trend-level notes.*
