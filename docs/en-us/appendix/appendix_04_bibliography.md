# Appendix IV: Bibliography and Further Reading

Sources consulted while writing this book, grouped by theme. One-line rationale per entry.

---

## I. Internet and Technology History

- ***The Wave of the Top*** (Wu Jun) — Rise and fall of century-scale tech firms from AT&T to Google.
- ***The Innovators*** (Walter Isaacson) — Collective biography of the digital revolution.
- ***Fire in the Valley*** (Michael Swaine & Paul Freiberger) — PC revolution origins.
- ***Bubbling Fifteen Years*** (Lin Jun) — Unofficial history of China's Internet 1995–2009.
- ***The Information*** (James Gleick) — How information shapes civilization.
- ***Where Wizards Stay Up Late*** (Katie Hafner) — Birth of the ARPANET and email.

---

## II. Business and Platform Economics

- ***Platform Strategy*** (Chen Weiru & Yu Zhuoxuan) — Two-sided markets and platform strategy. (See also Parker et al., *Platform Revolution*.)
- ***Zero to One*** (Peter Thiel) — Monopoly, innovation, contrarian startups.
- ***The Innovator's Dilemma*** (Clayton Christensen) — Disruptive innovation.
- ***The Cold Start Problem*** (Andrew Chen) — Network effects and overcoming cold start.
- ***Autonomous Driving Revolution*** — [Title/publication TBD by author] — Tesla, Waymo, OEM roadmaps.
- ***The Lean Startup*** (Eric Ries) — MVP and iteration.
- ***Competitive Strategy*** (Michael Porter) — Industry structure and five forces.

---

## III. AI and Compute

- ***Chip War*** (Chris Miller) — Semiconductors and geopolitics.
- ***AI 3.0*** (Melanie Mitchell) — Limits of today's AI.
- ***Life 3.0*** (Max Tegmark) — Superintelligence and the future.
- ***Deep Learning*** (Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville) — Technical reference.
- ***The Master Algorithm*** (Pedro Domingos) — Machine-learning paradigms.
- ***AI Superpowers*** (Kai-Fu Lee) — U.S.–China AI competition (early view).
- ***Human Compatible*** (Stuart Russell) — Alignment and safety.

---

## IV. Embodied Intelligence and Hardware

- ***Springer Handbook of Robotics*** (Siciliano et al.) — Robotics foundations.
- ***Power Battery Technology and Applications*** — [Citation TBD by author] — Lithium-ion supply chain, CATL, etc.
- **Boston Dynamics papers and videos** — Legged locomotion and control.
- **Smart manufacturing / IIoT white papers** — Foxconn, BYD automation.
- **IEEE Robotics journals** — Humanoids and embodied AI research.

---

## V. Economics Basics

- ***Principles of Economics*** (Mankiw) — Introductory micro/macro.
- ***Microeconomics*** (Pindyck) — Game theory and information economics.
- ***Innovation, Competition, and the Platform Economy*** (Jean Tirole) — Platform pricing and antitrust.
- ***Free*** (Chris Anderson) — Free business models online.
- ***The Long Tail*** (Chris Anderson) — Niche markets and infinite shelf space.

---

## VI. Web and Industry Reports

- **a16z blog** — Platforms, AI, crypto analysis.
- **Benedict Evans annual reviews** — Macro tech trends.
- **Stratechery** (Ben Thompson) — Aggregation theory and strategy.
- **IEEE Spectrum** — Chips, robots, AI news.
- **Wired / Ars Technica** — Product and policy coverage.
- **Company filings and investor days** — NVIDIA, Tesla, TSMC, etc.
- **Gartner / IDC** — Market sizing for cloud, AI, semiconductors.
